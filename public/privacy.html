HeroKana Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no cookies, and no personal data — ever. The installed app collects nothing at all. The free web version measures anonymous, aggregate usage only (which lessons get reached), with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.

What data HeroKana collects

The installed app (from Google Play) collects nothing — it has no backend and transmits no data. The free web version at herokana.netlify.app additionally sends anonymous, aggregate usage counts (see “Third-party services” below) so we can tell which parts of the course learners reach. No personal information, no accounts, and no cookies are involved on either version, and nothing you type is ever transmitted.

Where your learning progress lives

Your lesson progress, profile details you enter during onboarding (such as a display name), and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it using the built-in Backup feature, which saves a file to a location you choose.

Permissions

If you use the optional speaking practice, the app may request microphone access. Audio is processed entirely on your device (via your device's built-in speech services) and is never recorded to, or transmitted through, any HeroKana server — HeroKana has none.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts when first installed; after that it works fully offline. There is no advertising and there are no cross-site tracking SDKs.

The free web version (herokana.netlify.app) uses Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service that is cookieless and collects no personal data. It records only anonymous, aggregate events — for example, that some visitor finished the Hiragana chapter — so we can see where the course could be improved. It cannot identify you or track you across other sites. The installed app does not use analytics at all.

Children

HeroKana is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All app data is on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Reset progress" in Profile → Settings, removes it. There is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a sync feature is added), the updated policy will be posted at this address before the change ships.
