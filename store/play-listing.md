## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Learn to read Japanese — without the guilt trips.

HeroKana takes you from zero to reading real Japanese: hiragana, katakana, words, sentences, and real-world scenes like train stations, konbini, and cafés. No streaks to protect. No daily-goal nagging. No account to create. Just calm, focused lessons that respect your pace.

**A COMPLETE PATH, NOT JUST A CHART**
Most kana apps stop at the alphabet. HeroKana keeps going:
• Hiragana, katakana, voiced and combination kana — with memorable mnemonics
• 14 word and phrase themes, from greetings to travel
• Full sentences and grammar foundations
• Scenes: read station signs, order at restaurants, follow real conversations with audio
• A verb list and tap-to-hear kana chart for quick reference

**CALM BY DESIGN**
• No streaks, no countdown timers, no "you missed a day" guilt
• Gentle spaced review that resurfaces only what you actually missed
• One clear action per screen — the kana is the hero, not the interface

**YOURS, OFFLINE, PRIVATE**
• Works fully offline after install
• No account, no sign-up — start learning in seconds
• Zero data collection: your progress lives only on your device
• Free, no ads, no subscription

**PRACTICE YOUR WAY**
• Quick review, weak spots, speed rounds, and listening practice
• Hard mode with kanji prompts once you've finished the course
• Speaking practice in conversations — shadow native-paced audio

Whether you're preparing for a trip to Japan, starting your Japanese journey, or finally learning the kana you've been putting off — HeroKana is the quiet training partner that's happy to see you whenever you show up.
