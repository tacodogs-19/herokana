# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Japanese learners — beginners through early-intermediate — using this as a lightweight daily companion on mobile. Offline-first; used in short bursts (commute, break, quiet evening). No accounts, no sync, no commitment required to start.

## Product Purpose

HeroKana is a client-only PWA for learning Japanese kana, words, and sentences through structured lessons and spaced-repetition review. Success looks like a learner reaching the end of the chapter rail without feeling pressured or burned out.

## Brand Personality

Calm. Friendly. Focused. The app is a quiet training partner, not a gamified coach. It celebrates progress without nagging about absence.

## Anti-references

- Duolingo: no streaks, no daily-goal pressure, no loss-aversion mechanics, no "you missed a day" guilt.
- Typical flashcard apps: no bare grid of cards, no sterile clinical feel.
- Kids' edutainment: friendly, but not childish — the cat mascot is rationed tightly.

## Design Principles

1. **Calm over urgency.** Never use alarm-red, countdown timers, or streak pressure to motivate. Progress at the learner's pace.
2. **Content is the hero.** The kana glyph, not the interface chrome, should draw the eye. UI scaffolding is quiet.
3. **One confident action per screen.** Each card or screen has a single primary CTA. Secondary options are visually lighter.
4. **Mascot restraint.** The cat appears only on the icon, splash, onboarding, and results — never mid-flow.
5. **Light and dark are equals.** Both themes are hand-tuned, not auto-inverted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected globally — all keyframe animations collapse to instant.
- Safe-area insets honoured for notched Android/iOS PWA installs.
- WCAG AA contrast is the target; body text weight is 600 minimum to aid legibility.
